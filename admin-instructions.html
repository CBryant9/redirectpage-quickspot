Connect Quick Spot to Your HubSpot Account

How to Connect HubSpot

1
Send Connection Request to Your Admin
Send the following email to your HubSpot administrator to request access for Quick Spot:
Subject: Quick Spot HubSpot Connection Request
Hi [Admin Name], I'd like to use Quick Spot, a browser extension that helps streamline our HubSpot workflow. To connect it to our HubSpot account, I need admin approval. Could you please: 1. Click this link: [CONNECTION_LINK_HERE] 2. Authorize Quick Spot to access our HubSpot account Quick Spot is a productivity tool that helps with [brief description of what you'll use it for]. The connection is secure and follows HubSpot's OAuth standards. Let me know if you have any questions! Thanks, [Your Name]
Note: Replace [CONNECTION_LINK_HERE] with the actual connection URL provided by Quick Spot.
2
Wait for Admin Confirmation
Your administrator will receive your request and authorize the Quick Spot connection to your HubSpot account. This usually takes just a few minutes.
3
Connection Complete!
Once your admin confirms the connection, Quick Spot will automatically be linked to your HubSpot account. You can now start using all Quick Spot features with your HubSpot data.
