Install Quick Spot

Get started in less than a minute and start saving hours every week

1
Install the Chrome Extension

Click the button below to visit the Chrome Web Store and install Quick Spot. The installation takes just a few seconds.

Install from Chrome Web Store
Note: Quick Spot is currently available for Google Chrome and Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave, etc.)
2
Connect to Your HubSpot Account

After installation, click the Quick Spot icon in your browser toolbar. You'll be prompted to connect your HubSpot account. This secure connection allows Quick Spot to access your HubSpot data.

Privacy First: We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication. We never store your HubSpot password and you can revoke access at any time.
3
Create Your Custom Views

Set up your first custom view by selecting the HubSpot properties and relationships that matter most to you. You can create different views for different tasks or workflows.

Tip: Start with a simple view for your most common task, then create additional views as you get comfortable with the extension.
4
Start Using Quick Spot

You're all set! While browsing HubSpot, click the Quick Spot icon to instantly view your custom data views in a convenient side panel. No more tab-switching or endless scrolling.

Pro Tip: Use keyboard shortcuts to open Quick Spot even faster. Check the extension settings for available shortcuts.

Troubleshooting

🔧 Extension doesn't appear in toolbar

Click the puzzle piece icon in your Chrome toolbar and pin Quick Spot to make it always visible. You can also access it from chrome://extensions.

🔒 Can't connect to HubSpot

Make sure you're logged into HubSpot in the same browser. Check that your HubSpot account has the necessary permissions to access the data you need. If issues persist, try disconnecting and reconnecting your account.

⚠️ Data not loading properly

Try refreshing both HubSpot and the Quick Spot extension. Make sure your internet connection is stable. If you're using a VPN, some corporate VPNs may interfere with the connection.

🔄 Need to update subscription status

After completing payment setup through Stripe, click the Refresh button in the Quick Spot extension settings to update your subscription status.
