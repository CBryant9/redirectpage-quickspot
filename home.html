Associated Record's Made Easy

One of the biggest pains in HubSpot is that you can really only see one type of record in detail at a time. You're looking at a contact, but if you want to see multiple properties of the associated deals or multiple deals at once - you need to open another tab and click back and forth to get the whole picture.

With Quick Spot you can view your chosen record and all of its associated records on one page, and as it's an extension it's in a convenient side panel you can access and view without even needing to leave HubSpot.

Making this a powerful and convenient tool to add to your workflow and maximise your efficiency in finding the data you need.
